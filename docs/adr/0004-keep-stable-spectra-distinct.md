# Keep stable spectra distinct from unstable spaces

The cw49 corpus is represented by Conceptual spectra rather than by extending
Conceptual spaces with a stability flag. Stable cohomology operations do not
obey the unstable condition, one stable type can have infinitely many unstable
realizations, and the source corpus does not select canonical realizations;
future unstable records therefore remain independent and may connect through
an evidence-bearing Stabilization relation with an explicit suspension shift.

## Consequences

- Spectrum and space identifiers, names, assertions, Current projections, and
  atlas routes remain distinct even when an unstable realization is familiar.
- A stable Steenrod module never silently supplies operations for a Conceptual
  space, and suspension does not create aliases.
- The first cw49 release may record stable module data without inventing
  attaching maps, realization dimensions, or unstable families absent from the
  cited sources.
