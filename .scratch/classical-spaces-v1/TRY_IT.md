# Try the classical reference

Open [Homology Atlas](https://davearcher18.github.io/homology-db/) after the
classical release. No account, installation, or preparation is needed to browse.

1. Choose **CP²** from the home page. Read the ring and the degree of its
   generator. Follow **Compare with S² ∨ S⁴**: the additive groups match, but the
   square of the degree-two generator differs. Open the cup-product definition
   and a source citation.
2. Open **RP²**. Choose **F₂**, then **Q**. Both cohomology and homology update;
   compare the positive-degree groups and ring relations.
3. Open **Klein bottle** and choose **F₂**. Inspect the two degree-one generators
   and their squares. Compare the torus. Switch homology to **Reduced**: the
   ordinary cohomology ring must stay unchanged.

Try a space-name or notation search, browser Back, a narrow window, and a
different theme. Copy a page link or download its JSON to retain source context.

Coverage: 13 spaces over Q, F₂, F₃, F₅, F₇. All 42 existing spaces retain homology;
the separate stable preview keeps its existing links. Integral cohomology rings
and noncore rings explicitly say **Not recorded**. Ring records are sourced and
agent-checked; human mathematical review remains pending.
